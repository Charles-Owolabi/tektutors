Learning Hub access

Learning Hub accounts are created by the admin team.

Candidates do not create Learning Hub accounts directly. Access is only granted after the admin confirms that you have registered and meet the requirements for your class.

How access works

1. Register for your class with Tektutors.

2. The admin reviews your registration and confirms eligibility.

3. Approved candidates receive their Learning Hub login details from the admin.

4. Use those details on the sign-in page to access your classroom.

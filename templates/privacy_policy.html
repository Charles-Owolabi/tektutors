Tektutors Privacy Policy.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Tektutors.

Tektutors is committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of our students, prospective students, customers, instructors, website visitors, corporate clients, and other individuals who interact with our services.

This Privacy Policy explains how Tektutors collects, uses, stores, protects, and manages personal information when you visit our website, register for a training programme, contact us through WhatsApp or other communication channels, make enquiries, make payments, or otherwise interact with Tektutors.

By using our services or providing your personal information to us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Tektutors processes personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

3. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Tektutors, we may collect the following information.

A. Personal and Contact Information

This may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • WhatsApp number
  • Residential or business address where necessary
  • Country and city
  • Organisation or company name

B. Training and Educational Information

When you register for our training programmes, we may collect:

  • Course or programme selected
  • Learning preferences
  • Training schedule
  • Attendance information
  • Assignment or assessment information
  • Course progress
  • Certificates issued
  • Feedback and reviews

C. Payment and Transaction Information

When you make payments for our services, we may receive information necessary to confirm your transaction. Where payment processing is handled by a third-party payment provider, your payment card or banking information may be processed directly by that provider in accordance with its own privacy policy.

Tektutors does not intentionally request or store your complete payment card PIN, online banking password, or other highly sensitive banking credentials.

D. Communication Information

We may collect information you provide when you contact us through:

  • WhatsApp
  • Telephone calls
  • SMS
  • Email
  • Website contact forms
  • Social media platforms
  • Online training platforms
  • Other communication channels

This may include the content of your enquiry, feedback, requests, and communications.

4. Information Collected Through WhatsApp

Tektutors may use WhatsApp Business or WhatsApp Cloud API to communicate with customers, prospective students, trainees, instructors, and other users.

When you communicate with Tektutors through WhatsApp, we may process information such as:

  • Your WhatsApp name
  • Phone number
  • Messages sent to us
  • Information contained in your messages
  • Course enquiries
  • Registration information
  • Customer service requests
  • Communication history

We use this information to respond to enquiries, provide customer support, process registrations, communicate training schedules, send relevant service notifications, and manage our relationship with you.

WhatsApp is operated by Meta. Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp's applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. How We Use Your Information

Tektutors may use your personal information for legitimate business and educational purposes, including:

  1. Registering you for training programmes.
  2. Providing educational and training services.
  3. Communicating with you about your training.
  4. Responding to enquiries and customer support requests.
  5. Processing and confirming payments.
  6. Sending class schedules, reminders, and important announcements.
  7. Issuing certificates and maintaining training records.
  8. Improving our courses and services.
  9. Conducting surveys and collecting feedback.
  10. Managing our website and online platforms.
  11. Preventing fraud, abuse, or unauthorised activity.
  12. Complying with legal and regulatory obligations.
  13. Sending marketing communications where permitted and appropriate.
  14. Maintaining business and administrative records.

6. Marketing Communications

Where appropriate, Tektutors may send information about:

  • New courses
  • Training programmes
  • Discounts and promotions
  • Events and webinars
  • Special offers
  • Educational content
  • Business or professional development opportunities

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time. For WhatsApp communications, you may request that we stop sending promotional messages by contacting us or replying with a request such as STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE.

Please note that opting out of marketing messages does not necessarily prevent us from sending important service-related communications, such as registration confirmations, payment information, class changes, or other communications necessary to provide a service you have requested.

7. Legal Basis for Processing Personal Data

Depending on the circumstances, Tektutors may process your personal information based on one or more lawful grounds, including:

  • Your consent
  • Performance of a contract or provision of requested services
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Legitimate interests of Tektutors, where applicable
  • Other lawful grounds permitted under applicable data protection legislation

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent, subject to applicable legal limitations.

8. Sharing of Personal Information

Tektutors does not sell your personal information. We may share limited personal information with trusted third parties where necessary to provide our services or comply with legal obligations.

These may include:

  • Payment service providers
  • Website and hosting providers
  • Online learning platforms
  • Communication service providers
  • WhatsApp/Meta services
  • Cloud storage providers
  • Technology and software providers
  • Professional advisers
  • Government or regulatory authorities where legally required

We only share information where reasonably necessary and seek to use appropriate safeguards for protecting personal information.

Tektutors retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including:

  • Resolving disputes
  • Maintaining business records

When personal information is no longer required, we may securely delete, anonymise, or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with our applicable retention practices.
